# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Creator Growth Lab serves coaches, fitness creators, and personal brands who already publish useful, trust-building content and sell a real service, program, call, or coaching offer. They understand content, but do not want to learn Ads Manager, ROAS math, attribution, budget testing, or day-to-day campaign optimization.

## Product Purpose

Creator Growth Lab turns high-signal creator content into paid growth. The page should sell a done-for-you paid-growth service that finds promising posts, turns them into Meta ad tests, monitors performance, pauses weak tests, and moves budget toward winners. Success means more paid demand, more people ready to pay, and more real paying clients.

## Brand Personality

Warm, precise, and quietly technical. The brand should feel like a calm growth operator behind the creator, not a loud agency or a vague AI gimmick. It should lower the visitor's anxiety around ads while making the paid-growth system feel expert and credible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS pages, dark-blue tech dashboards, fake growth-hacking energy, vanity-metric bragging, vague lead language, and template agency pages with identical feature-card grids. Do not overpromise passive income, viral growth, or guaranteed outcomes.

## Design Principles

1. Sell the relief: the creator should feel they can grow without becoming a media buyer.
2. Make demand concrete: speak in paying clients, paid tests, and people ready to pay.
3. Show the operating system: make the agentic Meta ads layer tangible without turning the page into a dashboard.
4. Keep the page simple: one offer, one path, repeated CTA to Instagram.
5. Stay human: the systems are expert, but the service should feel approachable and accountable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-visible focus states, readable text on mobile, and reduced-motion support. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate meaning.
